# Animated Video Storyboard

## Project: "After Work" - Romantic Animation

---

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### **SCENE 1: The Arrival**
- **Duration**: 15-20 seconds
- **Setting**: Home entrance/hallway
- **Description**: She enters the quiet house first, tired but with anticipation
- **Key Elements**:
  - Close-up of her face, faint smile
  - Transition to freshening up
  - Mirror reflection moment
  - Soft lighting, warm tones
- **Visual Style**: Warm, intimate lighting
- **Audio**: Soft background music, subtle sound effects (door opening, water running)

---

### **SCENE 2: His Arrival**
- **Duration**: 10-15 seconds
- **Setting**: Front door
- **Description**: He comes home, exhausted but everything changes when he sees her
- **Key Elements**:
  - Door opening
  - His tired expression
  - Her quick change into comfortable clothes
  - Their eyes meeting
- **Visual Style**: Transition from darker (outside) to warm (inside)
- **Audio**: Music builds slightly, door sounds, subtle footsteps

---

### **SCENE 3: The Welcome**
- **Duration**: 15-20 seconds
- **Setting**: Hallway/living room entrance
- **Description**: She takes his bag without words; he sits, she brings water
- **Key Elements**:
  - Her taking his bag (domestic gesture)
  - Him sitting on sofa
  - His eyes following her
  - Her bringing water
  - Their connection through actions
- **Visual Style**: Soft focus on her movements, close-ups of their expressions
- **Audio**: Gentle music, subtle ambient sounds

---

### **SCENE 4: The Touch**
- **Duration**: 20-25 seconds
- **Setting**: Living room/sofa
- **Description**: He suddenly wraps his hand around her wrist, pulling her to his lap
- **Key Elements**:
  - Close-up of his hand around her wrist
  - Her gasp/surprise
  - Her landing on his lap
  - Both their shocked expressions
  - Her trembling hands
- **Visual Style**: Slightly faster cuts, intimate close-ups, warm lighting
- **Audio**: Music becomes more prominent, heartbeat sound effects

---

### **SCENE 5: The Hug**
- **Duration**: 15-20 seconds
- **Setting**: Sofa
- **Description**: A tight embrace—not rushed, just intimate and deeply needed
- **Key Elements**:
  - Close-up of their embrace
  - Facial expressions (her concern about door, his murmur)
  - Heartbeat emphasis (visual/audio)
  - His smile as he feels her heartbeat
- **Visual Style**: Soft, dreamy quality, intimate framing
- **Audio**: Heartbeat sounds, tender music swells

---

### **SCENE 6: Repositioning**
- **Duration**: 10-15 seconds
- **Setting**: Sofa
- **Description**: He guides her to sit beside him, then moves closer
- **Key Elements**:
  - Gentle movement from lap to beside him
  - Him moving closer
  - His hands lifting to her face
  - Eyes meeting
- **Visual Style**: Slow, deliberate movements, soft focus
- **Audio**: Music continues, subtle breathing sounds

---

### **SCENE 7: The Eye Contact**
- **Duration**: 10-15 seconds
- **Setting**: Sofa
- **Description**: Their eyes meet—everything else disappears
- **Key Elements**:
  - Close-up of their faces
  - Eye contact intensity
  - Internal monologue (her thoughts)
  - His confession: "I'm tired. And I want this... today."
- **Visual Style**: Close-ups, potential slow-motion effect, warm lighting
- **Audio**: Music peaks, soft dialogue/voiceover

---

### **SCENE 8: The Kiss - Approach**
- **Duration**: 15-20 seconds
- **Setting**: Sofa
- **Description**: Breaths mixing, hesitant approach to the kiss
- **Key Elements**:
  - Faces getting closer
  - Breath mixing (visual representation)
  - Their racing heartbeats
  - Anticipation building
  - Initial soft contact
- **Visual Style**: Slow, intimate, close-ups of faces
- **Audio**: Music building, heartbeat sounds, gentle breathing

---

### **SCENE 9: The Kiss - Deepening**
- **Duration**: 20-30 seconds
- **Setting**: Sofa
- **Description**: The kiss deepens—full of passion and missing
- **Key Elements**:
  - Intensifying kiss
  - Her fingers gripping his shirt
  - His hands on her waist
  - Growing intensity
  - Loss of awareness of surroundings
- **Visual Style**: Dynamic angles, warm lighting, possibly slight motion blur
- **Audio**: Music crescendo, heartbeat intensifies, passionate tension

---

### **SCENE 10: The Interruption**
- **Duration**: 5-10 seconds
- **Setting**: Sofa
- **Description**: Doorbell rings—moment broken
- **Key Elements**:
  - Freeze moment
  - Both stop, breathing hard
  - Foreheads touching
  - Shock of interruption
- **Visual Style**: Abrupt shift—could use sound effect emphasis
- **Audio**: Sharp doorbell sound, music cuts/pauses

---

### **SCENE 11: The Resolution**
- **Duration**: 15-20 seconds
- **Setting**: Sofa
- **Description**: They freeze, then share knowing looks and smiles
- **Key Elements**:
  - Her shaky breath (nervous/amused)
  - His slow, intimate smile
  - Still holding each other close
  - Unwillingness to let go
  - Fade to black with final line
- **Visual Style**: Warm, intimate, satisfied expression
- **Audio**: Music softens, subtle smile/breath sounds, voiceover closing line: "Some moments, even unfinished, stay burning."

---

## Visual Style Guide

### Color Palette
- **Dominant Colors**: Warm oranges, golds, deep reds, soft pinks
- **Accent Colors**: Deep blues (evening outside), soft whites
- **Overall Tone**: Warm, intimate, romantic

### Lighting
- **Interior**: Warm, soft lighting (lamps, natural sunset)
- **Shadows**: Gentle, creating depth but not darkness
- **Special Moments**: Slightly glowing effect during intimate moments

### Character Design Notes
- **Both Characters**: Adult, professional appearance
- **Her**: Delicate features, expressive eyes, graceful movements
- **Him**: Strong features, tired but warm expression, confident movements
- **Both**: Should convey deep emotional connection through body language

---

## Audio Design

### Music
- **Tempo**: Start slow and build gradually
- **Instruments**: Piano (primary), strings (violins, cellos), subtle orchestral elements
- **Emotional Arc**: Tender → Building → Passionate → Interrupted → Satisfied

### Sound Effects
- Door opening/closing
- Water sounds (shower)
- Footsteps (subtle)
- Glass clinking
- Heartbeat (emphasis at key moments)
- Doorbell (sharp, sudden)
- Breathing (intimate moments)

### Voiceover/Dialogue
- Internal monologue: "Why does he look at me like that? Like I'm the only thing he wants."
- His confession: "I'm tired. And I want this… today."
- Whispered dialogue: "Someone might come..." / "Stay quiet..."
- Final line: "Some moments, even unfinished, stay burning."

**Estimated**: 3-4 minutes (detailed animation)

## Production Notes
- Animation style: 2D or 3D (recommend 2D for emotional intimacy)
- Frame rate: 24fps (cinematic)
- Resolution: 1080p minimum (1440p or 4K preferred)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (widescreen)
