# Production Plan - "After Work" Animation

## Project Overview
Converting the romantic narrative "After Work" into a 3-4 minute animated video.

---

## Production Timeline

### **Phase 1: Pre-Production** (Weeks 1-2)
- [ ] Finalize animation style and aesthetic
- [ ] Create detailed character designs
- [ ] Design background/locations (apartment layout)
- [ ] Color palette finalization
- [ ] Storyboard refinement with visual sketches
- [ ] Record voiceover/narration
- [ ] Commission music or source stock music

**Deliverables**:
- Character design sheets
- Background concept art
- Visual storyboard (sketches)
- Audio files (voiceover, music)

---

### **Phase 2: Production** (Weeks 3-8)
- [ ] Clean up/finalize character animations
- [ ] Animate all scenes (one by one)
- [ ] Layer backgrounds and effects
- [ ] Add lighting and shadow effects
- [ ] Implement transitions between scenes
- [ ] Color correction and grading

**Breakdown by Scene**:
- Scene 1-2: 1 week
- Scene 3-5: 1.5 weeks
- Scene 6-7: 1 week
- Scene 8-9: 1.5 weeks (most complex)
- Scene 10-11: 0.5 weeks
- Effects & Refinements: 1 week

**Deliverables**:
- Completed animated scenes
- Draft video cuts

---

### **Phase 3: Post-Production** (Weeks 9-10)
- [ ] Final video assembly
- [ ] Sound design and audio mixing
- [ ] Add visual effects (glows, particles, etc.)
- [ ] Color grading pass
- [ ] Visual effects enhancement
- [ ] Quality check and revisions

**Deliverables**:
- Final video file (multiple formats)
- Behind-the-scenes content

---

### **Phase 4: Release & Distribution** (Week 11+)
- [ ] Upload to platforms (YouTube, Vimeo, etc.)
- [ ] Social media promotion
- [ ] Community feedback collection
- [ ] Consider extended cuts or alternate versions

---

## Animation Tools & Software Options

### **Recommended Option: Adobe Animate + Blender**
- **Adobe Animate**: For 2D character animation
- **Blender**: For 3D backgrounds and lighting
- **Adobe Premiere Pro**: For video editing and assembly
- **Cost**: $20-55/month subscription

### **Alternative Option: Krita + Blender (Free)**
- **Krita**: Open-source digital painting and animation
- **Blender**: Free 3D animation
- **DaVinci Resolve**: Free video editing
- **Cost**: Free (donations welcome)

### **Premium Option: Toon Boom Harmony**
- Industry standard for professional 2D animation
- Cost**: $300-500/month
- **Best for**: Professional-quality output

### **AI-Assisted Option: Runway ML + Adobe Tools**
- AI-powered video generation
- Reduces manual animation time
- **Cost**: $15-50/month

## Budget Estimate

### **Minimal Budget** (DIY with free tools)
- Software: Free (Krita, Blender, DaVinci Resolve)
- Music: $0-50 (royalty-free or commissioned)
- Voice: $0-100 (DIY or freelancer)
- **Total**: $0-150

### **Mid-Range Budget** (Adobe Suite)
- Software: $120 (3 months of Creative Cloud)
- Music: $50-200 (commissioned composer or high-quality stock)
- Voice: $100-300 (professional voice actor)
- **Total**: $270-620

### **Professional Budget** (Toon Boom + Full Services)
- Software: $1,200-1,500 (3-4 months)
- Professional Voice Actor: $300-500
- Composer: $500-1,000
- **Total**: $2,000-3,000

## Technical Specifications

### **Video Output**
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum, 2560x1440 preferred
- **Frame Rate**: 24fps (cinematic standard)
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264) for streaming, ProRes 422 for archival
- **Color Space**: sRGB for web, DCI-P3 for cinema
- **Audio**: Stereo or 5.1 Surround (if budget allows)

### **File Size Estimates**
- 1080p MP4 (3:30 min): 200-500 MB
- 1440p MP4 (3:30 min): 400-800 MB
- 4K MP4 (3:30 min): 800-1.5 GB
